Book of SOPs · For Agents

🤝 Client Situations

What this is: Not sure how to handle a difficult client? Start here.

The 4 Golden Rules

  1. Never respond in the same emotional state — wait, breathe, then respond
  2. Never put anything in writing you wouldn't want read in a courtroom
  3. Never diagnose a defect — always recommend inspection
  4. Make note of the interaction and pin it to the top of the client's file
✅ Handle it yourself — just give Team Lead a recap within 24 hours ⌄
IF: Buyer is unhappy with a property after a showing → DO: Acknowledge feelings, refocus the search. "That's really helpful feedback — let's use that to narrow in on what will feel right."
IF: Seller wants to know why there haven't been showings yet → DO: Review marketing activity, provide showing data, reassure. "Here's what we've done this week — let's talk about what we adjust next."
IF: Client is pushing for a faster response → DO: Set clear communication expectations. "I'll always get back to you within [X] hours — if it's urgent, call or text me directly."
IF: Client asks a question you don't know the answer to → DO: "That's a great question — let me get you the right answer rather than guess. I'll have that for you by end of day."
⚠️ Slack Team Lead before responding — do not reply to the client yet ⌄
IF: Post-closing complaint about property condition → DO: Do not admit fault. Get specifics in writing. Team Lead advises on response and whether title company involvement is needed.
IF: Client requests a credit or financial concession → DO: Never commit to a number. "Let me look into this and get back to you today." Team Lead approves any credit offered.
IF: Client is threatening to leave or cancel → DO: Do not panic or make promises. Team Lead advises on retention strategy or professional exit.
IF: Inspection dispute — buyer wants repairs or credits → DO: Get contractor quotes before agreeing to anything. Team Lead caps expectation. Standard threshold: $1,000–$1,500 credit before escalating further.
IF: Seller disagrees with pricing strategy → DO: Bring market data. "The market is telling us something — let me show you what I'm seeing." Team Lead supports pricing conversations if needed.
🚨 Stop. Notify Team Lead immediately and step back. ⌄
IF: Client becomes verbally abusive or threatening → DO: Disengage professionally. "I want to help resolve this — let me bring in our team lead." Team Lead takes over.
IF: Post-closing harassment (repeated hostile contact) → DO: Stop responding. Team Lead advises on disengagement and whether legal counsel is needed.
IF: Client accuses agent of misrepresentation or fraud → DO: Do not respond. Contact Team Lead immediately. Real Broker legal may be involved.
IF: Fair housing concern raised by any party → DO: Do not respond. Contact Team Lead immediately. This is a brokerage-level issue.
IF: Media or public complaint (social media, review) → DO: Do not respond publicly. Screenshot and send to Team Lead. Team Lead drafts any public response.
