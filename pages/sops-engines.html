Section A

Deal of the Week

Submit a horse property to Trello by Wednesday EOD. Catherine sends the email Thursday.

Non-Negotiable

Deadline: End of Day Wednesday. No exceptions.

For Agents — How to Submit ⌄
  1. Find a property on MLS, Zillow, or another source — horse property or a property with horse property potential
  2. Go to the team Trello board, find the Deal of the Week list
  3. Add a card with the property address
  4. Label the card with your name, so the coordinator knows who submitted it
  5. Done — the coordinator handles everything from there
For Coordinators — How to Send It ⌄
  1. Pull the property card from the Trello DOTW list Wednesday or Thursday morning
  2. Go to Zillow or the listing source, copy all property info — address, price, specs, description, photos
  3. Paste the full info into the PLP Custom GPT, which drafts the email
  4. Review the draft, make any necessary edits
  5. Send the email to the DOTW subscriber list on Thursday
Section B

Open Houses

Run every open house the same way, every time.

2+ Days Before ⌄
  1. Agent Schedule the open house, notify Catherine (date, time, address)
  2. Agent Coordinate with the listing agent to get it entered on the MLS (if not your listing)
  3. Agent Request Catherine build the Luxury Presence sign-in landing page for this property
  4. Agent Create a social graphic or short video — date/time, property website link, location
  5. Agent Post the announcement on Facebook, Instagram, and all approved local Facebook groups — include the property link
  6. Catherine Confirm it's included in the team's weekly database email once notified
Day Before ⌄
  1. Confirm the Luxury Presence sign-in page is live and working — test it on your own device
  2. Print MLS sheets with PinkLady branding (approved template)
  3. Print the Real Estate Condition Report (RECR)
  4. Print the lender list
  5. Gather business cards — bring more than you think you need
  6. Confirm directional sign locations — drive the route if needed
  7. Charge your tablet, confirm the sign-in page loads correctly on it
  8. Bring or confirm a Bluetooth speaker for background music
Day Of — Setup ⌄
  1. Place directional signs on the route to the property (minimum 2 signs)
  2. Arrive at the property 30 minutes before the start time
  3. Turn on all lights, every room
  4. Walk the property — clean, presentable, well-staged
  5. Start light background music, low volume
  6. Set up your tablet with the sign-in page open and ready
  7. Set out printed materials — MLS sheets, RECR, lender list, business cards
During the Open House ⌄
  1. Greet every visitor at the door — no one enters without signing in
  2. Use the exact sign-in script every time (below)
  3. Direct visitors to sign in on the tablet — auto-tags them "Open House" in Lofty
  4. Immediately confirm you're tagged as lead owner in Lofty — don't let leads go unassigned
  5. After sign-in, let visitors explore freely — don't follow room to room
  6. Engage with open-ended questions: "What brought you out today?" / "What are you looking for?" / "What did you think of the property?"
  7. Stay friendly, professional, available — present without hovering
Sign-In Script — Memorize This
"Our sellers have requested that everyone sign in before entering their home."
Right After ⌄
  1. Count total visitors who signed in
  2. If not your listing — call/text the listing agent immediately with attendance count + notable feedback
  3. If your own listing — prepare to deliver attendance + feedback to sellers (same day or next morning at latest)
  4. Collect all directional signs
  5. Return the property to its original state — lights off, everything as you found it
Within 24 Hours ⌄
  1. Send the follow-up email to every visitor who signed in, using the approved template — ask for feedback to pass to sellers
  2. Check Lofty — confirm all sign-in leads captured, tagged "Open House," and assigned to you
  3. Add a follow-up task in Lofty for each new lead — don't let them sit without a next action
If This → Do This
IF: A lead from your open house ends up assigned to someone else because you didn't check Lofty within 24 hours → DO: Nothing — that's on you. No CRM tag, no claim.
