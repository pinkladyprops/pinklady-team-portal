Section 1

Getting Started

The application steps every new agent completes before joining.

View the steps ⌄
  1. Research Real Brokerage — review the business model and culture
  2. Connect with your sponsor — the Real agent who most influenced your decision to join. This cannot be changed after joining, so be sure before you commit
  3. Confirm your real estate license is active and in good standing in your state
  4. Complete the online application at Real, selecting your sponsor during the process
  5. Review and sign the Independent Contractor Agreement — read it carefully, and confirm your sponsor's name is listed correctly, as this cannot be changed later
Section 2

After You Apply

What happens once your application is in.

View the steps ⌄
  1. Watch for onboarding emails from Real
  2. Complete required onboarding training
  3. Set up your Real dashboard and profile
  4. Explore Lofty CRM training and support
  5. Join Real's Workplace community
  6. Upload your W9 and any required compliance documents
  7. Schedule a meeting with your sponsor
Section 3

Transferring from Another Brokerage

Only if applicable.

Skip this section if this is your first real estate license.

View the steps ⌄
  1. Complete your Real Application — sign the ICA and pay the application fee.
    You'll need:
    • License number and expiration date
    • MLS and board information
    • Sponsor name and email
    • Driver's license photo
  2. Transfer your license — your Onboarding Coordinator sends Form 812 electronically. Register on the WI DSPS Self Serve portal, upload the form, pay the transfer fee, then email proof of payment to onboarding@therealbrokerage.com
  3. Notify your local board and MLS of the transfer
  4. Update all marketing to include "Real Broker" or "Real Broker, LLC" per Wisconsin advertising requirements
  5. Transfer active listings — submit transfer forms electronically
  6. Register for core training sessions (Real Onboarding, Transaction Training, Technology, Marketing, Benefits & Programs, Ways to Earn Income)
  7. Join the Workvivo Wisconsin State Space and download the reZEN app
