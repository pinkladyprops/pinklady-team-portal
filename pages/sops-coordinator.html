Section A

Lofty AI Guardrails

What automation is approved to run, and what to do when something goes wrong.

Warning

No automation may run unattended without Team Lead's explicit written approval.

✅ Approved to Run ⌄
  • Auto Property Alert — sends matching new listings to leads on a set schedule
  • Zapier sync (Lofty → Constant Contact) — contact sync only, no messaging
  • Client Consult booking page — auto-generates Meet link and blocks calendar
🚫 Turn Off Immediately ⌄
  • AI Voice channel ("Unknown Inbound Calls") — running overnight 10pm–8am. Turn off today.
  • Any AI feature that auto-creates leads without human review
  • Any AI feature that contacts a client or prospect without human approval of the message
  • Follow Up Boss integration toggle — unconfirmed if in active use. Disable until confirmed.
🔥 If an Automation Misbehaves → What to Do ⌄
IF: An automation misbehaves → DO:
  1. Turn it off immediately — do not wait to investigate first
  2. Notify Team Lead via Slack within 15 minutes: "Turned off [feature] — it [describe what it did]. Waiting on your direction."
  3. Document what happened in Lofty notes or a Google Doc — what fired, when, what it did, who was affected
  4. Do not turn it back on without Team Lead's explicit approval
Section B

📄 Document Send Authorization

What the coordinator can send without asking — and what always needs Team Lead approval first.

✅ Send Without Asking ⌄
Showing & Appointment Confirmations
  • Showing confirmation emails to buyers — standard template
  • Calendar invite with property address, time, and Meet link
  • Appointment confirmation to seller with time and agent name
  • Reminder emails sent 24 hours before any scheduled appointment
Property & Listing Information
  • MLS listing sheets to buyers who have requested a property
  • Real Estate Condition Reports (RECR) sent with showing confirmations
  • Coming Soon or Active listing announcements to the database
  • Deal of the Week email — once Team Lead has approved the property selection
  • Weekly update email — Tuesday tips, Friday market update (pre-approved templates)
Routine Transaction Documents
  • Earnest money receipt confirmation to buyer once check is received
  • Inspection scheduling confirmation to all parties once time is agreed
  • Title company introduction email — standard template only
  • Closing date/time confirmation once all parties have agreed
  • Post-closing thank you email — standard template
Administrative & Onboarding
  • New agent welcome email and onboarding checklist
  • Tech setup instructions and login credential emails
  • Standard vendor invoices for pre-approved recurring vendors
  • BizBox orders under $200 for active listings — no approval needed
🚫 Always Ask Team Lead First ⌄
Anything Involving Money or Terms
  • Any amendment to price, dates, or contract terms — regardless of how minor it seems
  • Commission disbursement authorization or split adjustments
  • Vendor invoices over $200 or any invoice from a non-recurring vendor
  • Any document requesting a credit, concession, or price reduction
  • Earnest money release or disbursement instructions
Anything With Legal Implications
  • Offer to Purchase — any version, any stage
  • Counteroffers — any version
  • Inspection response or repair request documents
  • Any addendum that changes contract terms
  • Termination or cancellation documents
  • Any document going to a title company for closing purposes
  • Delayed Addendum — must verify MLS visibility settings before sending
Anything Involving a Difficult Client Situation
  • Any communication to a client who has expressed frustration or complaint
  • Any response to a post-closing dispute or demand
  • Any communication where the tone needs to be carefully managed
  • Any situation where you are unsure — when in doubt, ask
  • Any communication involving a potential legal dispute

How to Submit for Approval

  1. Draft the document or email completely
  2. Send to Team Lead via Slack: "Ready to send: [document name] to [recipient]. Waiting on your go-ahead."
  3. Do not send until Team Lead replies Yes or Go ahead
  4. If no response within 2 hours and time-sensitive, follow up once in Slack

Section D

Deal of the Week — Coordinator Steps

Submit a horse property to Trello by Wednesday EOD. Catherine sends the email Thursday.

Non-Negotiable

Deadline: End of Day Wednesday. No exceptions.

How to Send It ⌄
  1. Pull the property card from the Trello DOTW list Wednesday or Thursday morning
  2. Go to Zillow or the listing source, copy all property info — address, price, specs, description, photos
  3. Paste the full info into the PLP Custom GPT, which drafts the email
  4. Review the draft, make any necessary edits
  5. Send the email to the DOTW subscriber list on Thursday
