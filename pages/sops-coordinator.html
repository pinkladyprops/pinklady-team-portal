Section A

Lofty AI Guardrails

What automation is approved to run, and what to do when something goes wrong.

Warning

No automation may run unattended without Cailyn's explicit written approval.

✅ Approved to Run ⌄
  • Auto Property Alert — sends matching new listings to leads on a set schedule
  • Zapier sync (Lofty → Constant Contact) — contact sync only, no messaging
  • Client Consult booking page — auto-generates Meet link and blocks calendar
🚫 Turn Off Immediately ⌄
  • AI Voice channel ("Unknown Inbound Calls") — running overnight 10pm–8am. Turn off today.
  • Any AI feature that auto-creates leads without human review
  • Any AI feature that contacts a client or prospect without human approval of the message
  • Follow Up Boss integration toggle — unconfirmed if in active use. Disable until confirmed.
🔥 If an Automation Misbehaves → What to Do ⌄
IF: An automation misbehaves → DO:
  1. Turn it off immediately — do not wait to investigate first
  2. Notify Cailyn via Slack within 15 minutes: "Turned off [feature] — it [describe what it did]. Waiting on your direction."
  3. Document what happened in Lofty notes or a Google Doc — what fired, when, what it did, who was affected
  4. Do not turn it back on without Cailyn's explicit approval

Section C

Deal of the Week — Coordinator Steps

Submit a horse property to Trello by Wednesday EOD. Catherine sends the email Thursday.

Non-Negotiable

Deadline: End of Day Wednesday. No exceptions.

How to Send It ⌄
  1. Pull the property card from the Trello DOTW list Wednesday or Thursday morning
  2. Go to Zillow or the listing source, copy all property info — address, price, specs, description, photos
  3. Paste the full info into the PLP Custom GPT, which drafts the email
  4. Review the draft, make any necessary edits
  5. Send the email to the DOTW subscriber list on Thursday
