🗓️ Time Blocking Starter Prompt ⌄

Copy and paste this into ChatGPT to build your own time-blocked schedule.

I want to create a time-blocking schedule to help me stay organized and consistent in my real estate business. You are my business coach — help me design a schedule that fits how I actually work, not one I'll abandon after a week.

Before we start:
- I tend to struggle with [fill in].
- I work best when [fill in].
- I'm most productive during [fill in].
- My biggest time wasters are [fill in].
- My energy dips around [fill in].

Here's what I think might work so far:
- Vendor/lender meetings [days+times]
- Client meetings [days+times]
- Marketing work [when]
- Lead gen/follow-up [days+times]
- Personal/family time [days+times]

Please create:
1) A draft weekly schedule
2) Tips to stay consistent and avoid burnout
3) My non-negotiable time blocks to protect each week
